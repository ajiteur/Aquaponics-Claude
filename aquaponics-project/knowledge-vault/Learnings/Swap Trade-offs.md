# Swap Trade-offs

Generated by `python scripts/compare_swaps.py --vault` from the swap runs (`runs/swaps_basil_nl.json`, `runs/swaps_basil_karnataka.json`). Do not edit by hand.

**Scenario estimate, not validated.** Each swap changes one choice in a 100 m2 tilapia + basil raft build (owner labour, mid case) and is compared with that base. Gains and losses come from the configurator's numbers; risk and labour notes come from the catalog. Missing values stay missing. Values marked pending are candidates awaiting approval.

## Netherlands (heated glasshouse)

Base: EUR 9.15 / 11.68 / 14.55 per kg of basil (low / mid / high cost).

### Media bed with expanded clay instead of DWC raft

- **You gain:** EUR 142 less cost per m2 of grow area per year; 13,797 kWh less electricity per year; 230 m3 less top-up water per year.
- **You give up:** EUR 6,311 more to build; 1,327 kg less fish per year; no sourced basil yield, so no cost per kg.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Beds clog with solids and need labour-intensive washing (FAO 589); the media doubles as solids filter and biofilter in small systems. Washing clogged media; no raft handling.
- **Left out of the cost (not like-for-like):** media_expanded_clay_nl: lifetime; plant yield for basil in media_bed (no sourced value; set species_overrides.plant.yield_kg_per_m2_yr); water held in the media_bed grow unit (not sourced; system water counts tanks and filters only).
- **Confidence:** low. Feed and fish fall to 25% of the raft rate on one SRAC sentence (low confidence); no sourced media-bed basil yield; clay lifetime and bed container not sourced.
- **Note:** Fish and feed drop to 25% of the raft rate (SRAC 454 factor, low confidence). No sourced media-bed basil yield, so cost per kg is n/a; bed container not priced. Separate K1 biofilter media added (one German listing, candidate), as the configurator rules ask for media beds.

### NFT with uPVC channels instead of DWC raft

- **You gain:** EUR 5.32 less cost per kg of basil; EUR 7,659 less to build; 13,797 kWh less electricity per year; 230 m3 less top-up water per year.
- **You give up:** 272 kg less basil per year; 1,327 kg less fish per year.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Most sensitive to pump stops: a thin film dries out within hours, while a raft holds water. Needs fine solids removal and a separate biofilter. Channels need flushing and root clearing after each crop.
- **Left out of the cost (not like-for-like):** NFT channel metres per m2 of grow area (no sourced layout; set design_overrides.nft_channel_m_per_m2); nft_channel_upvc_100x80_nl: sizing driver nft_channel_length_m; water held in the nft grow unit (not sourced; system water counts tanks and filters only).
- **Confidence:** low. NFT yield is 0.83-0.95 of raft from two studies that disagree on size (pending Batch 003); fish feed at 25% of the raft rate is a low-confidence rule; channels and biofilter not fully priced.
- **Note:** Yield = DWC yield x 0.83-0.95 (Blunk 2023, Pasch 2021; candidate). Channel metres per m2 not sourced, so channel cost is a gap. The separate biofilter NFT needs uses K1 media from one German listing (candidate); the vessel is not priced.

### Air-water heat pump instead of gas boiler (same heat demand)

- **You gain:** EUR 2.04 less cost per kg of basil.
- **You give up:** EUR 9,679 more to build; 12,524 kWh more electricity per year.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Needs a low-temperature (35-45 C) heating system; cost depends on the gas-to-power price ratio. Service contract and refrigerant checks (frequency not sourced).
- **Left out of the cost (not like-for-like):** heating_heat_pump_air_water_nl: lifetime.
- **Confidence:** medium. COP 3.7-4.6 and gas-to-heat conversion are corroborated Tier 2 values (pending Batch 003), but heat demand is transferred from sweet pepper and the heat pump lifetime is not sourced.
- **Note:** Heat pump lifetime is not sourced, so its build cost per year is left out. Gas boiler price is also missing in the base.

### Unheated glasshouse (heating off)

- **You gain:** EUR 3.44 less cost per kg of basil.
- **You give up:** nothing measurable in the model.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Basil stops growing or dies in cold months.
- **Confidence:** low. The model keeps the heated yield, but an unheated Dutch glasshouse would not grow basil through winter (not modelled), so this is an upper bound on the heating cost, not a real option.
- **Note:** Upper bound on what heating costs. Yield stays at the heated value, which an unheated NL house would not reach in winter (not modelled).

### Existing building instead of a new glasshouse

### grow_unit: EPS board 40 mm as rafts instead of dwc_raft_xps_30mm_nl

- **You gain:** EUR 0.03 less cost per kg of basil; EUR 628 less to build.
- **You give up:** nothing measurable in the model.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Rafts must be washed between crops. Cheapest raft; EPS beads crumble and soak water, so it wears faster and is harder to sanitise. Homemade EPS rafts absorb water and break under load (USDA-NIFA); XPS carries fire retardants, a food-contact concern.
- **Confidence:** low. Single listing.

### grow_unit: Raft trough walls: impregnated pine planks 28x195 mm instead of dwc_trough_walls_nl

- **You gain:** lifetime 10-15 years instead of 10 years.
- **You give up:** EUR 0.02 more cost per kg of basil; EUR 605 more to build.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Check for rot where the liner folds over. Easy to build without masonry; about twice the block price per m2. Timber in permanent contact with damp may rot sooner than the seller's claim.
- **Confidence:** low. Single listing; per-plank price assumed (pack not explicit).

### grow_unit: Raft trough walls: hollow concrete blocks 39x19x19 cm instead of dwc_trough_walls_nl

- **Confidence:** low. Belgian listing used for the Netherlands; VAT unstated.

### water_pump: Aquaforte DM-10000 pond pump (10,000 L/h, 85 W) instead of pump_aquaforte_dme_10000_nl

- **You gain:** nothing measurable in the model.
- **You give up:** EUR 0.02 more cost per kg of basil; EUR 129 more to build; 88 kWh more electricity per year.
- **Risk, labour and practice:** Clean intake screen. Slightly more flow for more watts and a higher price; discontinued.
- **Confidence:** low. Discontinued item.

### structure: No new structure (existing building or open site) instead of structure_venlo_glasshouse_nl

- **You gain:** EUR 0.34 less cost per kg of basil; EUR 13,244 less to build.
- **You give up:** nothing measurable in the model.

- **Confidence:** high. Zero by definition; the real cost is the unpriced space.
