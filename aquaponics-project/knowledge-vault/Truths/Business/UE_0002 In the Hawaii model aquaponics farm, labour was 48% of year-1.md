# UE_0002

> In the Hawaii model aquaponics farm, labour was 48% of year-1 operating cost, and the return was most sensitive to lettuce price, then wage.

**Candidate truth.** Not yet approved (`candidate_pending_user_review`).

## Context

- system: Coupled DWC raceway model farm, 4 x 5,000 gal tanks, 12,288 sq ft (about 1,142 m2) raceway
- crop: lettuce plus tilapia
- location: Hawaii, tropical
- year basis: fieldwork 2012, USD

## Reliability

- Source tier: T3_institutional_report
- Confidence: medium
- Confidence reason: Representative model farm with a documented sensitivity analysis; USD 2012 prices.
- Cross check: Labour as the largest cost agrees with UVI (Heidemann and Bailey 2015) and Purdue (Quagrainie et al.).
