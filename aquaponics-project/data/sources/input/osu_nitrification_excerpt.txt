For aquaponics, a pH range of 6.5 to 7.5 should be maintained. Plants absorb some nutrients better at slightly acidic pH ranges, while microbes nitrify better at slightly to moderately alkaline pH ranges. Therefore, a moderate pH range of 6.8 to 7.2 is often recommended, but may be too restrictive for all situations and systems. One of several processes in the system that affects pH is nitrification. As microbes convert ammonia to nitrate H ions are released, thus lowering pH. In many systems, this makes it necessary to add a base (potassium hydroxide and calcium hydroxide).
